## 🎮 About the Game

Released on August 31, 2026, Sucker for Love: Crush Landing is a horror-comedy romance visual novel developed by Akabaka and published by Black Lantern Collective. It combines point-and-click exploration, colorful item puzzles, branching dialogue, multiple endings, and RPG-style progression in a hand-drawn 2D apartment setting.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Freeze Event Timer** — Stops the countdown for timed story events, giving you unlimited time to explore, answer the door, make phone calls, and solve puzzles.
- **Restore Hheily's Flame** — Restores Hheily's flame and prevents it from being extinguished while you experiment with objects and color combinations.
- **Instant Puzzle Progress** — Completes selected flame-feeding or color-based progression requirements without requiring repeated trial and error.
- **Protect Story Progress** — Reduces the risk of losing progress while testing dialogue choices, door interactions, phone calls, and alternate routes.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Item Use** — Allows inventory objects to be used repeatedly without permanently consuming them.
- **Preserve Item States** — Prevents important objects from being lost or permanently altered after they are used in the apartment's puzzles.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Combat Mode** — Crush Landing has no conventional combat system; this trainer focuses on its timed events, survival pressure, inventory puzzles, and branching story decisions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

The game uses point-and-click interaction in hand-drawn 2D apartment environments. Move between available areas with the mouse, inspect objects, and select interactive hotspots. The trainer's timer option is useful when you want to examine every room and object before a timed event occurs.

### Tools & Inventory

Objects found around the apartment can be collected and used to maintain Hheily's flame or solve progression puzzles. Item color and function matter, and some objects may be consumed or change after use. Enable Unlimited Item Use or Preserve Item States before testing combinations that are difficult to replace.

### World & Environment

The apartment is the central gameplay space, with additional story interactions triggered through the front door, telephone, letters, and other environmental objects. Timed visits and conversations can advance the story more quickly, so Freeze Event Timer is recommended for completing optional interactions and exploring alternate choices.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no traditional combat system. The main threats come from the fading flame, timed events, dangerous choices, and story consequences. Restore Hheily's Flame and Freeze Event Timer provide control over these pressures while leaving dialogue and route selection to the player.
